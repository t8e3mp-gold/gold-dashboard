# Recommendation rules (R5). Computed, display only, no AI text, no orders.

Inputs: levels (zones, ATR), news (wait window, leak/trap flag, next event), backtest (out-of-sample results per rule). Spot only, own the asset, buy dips and sell rallies in tranches. No leverage or shorting.

1. Staged gate (user decision, Nov 28 2026). Stages are 60, 70, 80, 90 and 98 (% win rate, out of sample, after fees). A stage opens for a coin only if some rule has at least 200 out-of-sample trades (the 60 stage needs 100, user decision Nov 28 2026, 04:00), a win rate at or above the stage, and a positive total net after fees. Trades that ended by the 48 h time stop ("stuck") and trades still open when the data ends (marked at the last close) are inside those numbers, and the page shows how many there were. The 98 stage also needs a maximum drawdown under 20%. Stages 60 to 90 mean "under observation" and are never a recommendation. Only the 98 stage is the full recommendation level and the only one that lets the stance leave "wait". Until a stage opens the page says "no proven edge". The best rule is still shown with its numbers. These are backtest (simulated) numbers; the forward log (measured) is the real check. Replaces the earlier rule (100 trades and a positive average).
2. Wait overrides. A scheduled-event window (2 h before to 1 h after) or a possible leak/trap flag forces "wait". The official release is confirmation, not the first signal.
3. Only at the 98 stage and quiet news: "buy watch" if price is within 1 ATR above the nearest support and a bounce is forming; "sell watch" if price is within 1 ATR below the nearest resistance. Otherwise "wait".
4. Conditional plans (always shown, never a call): buy zone = strongest support within 5% (else nearest); confirmation = a 1h close above the zone; invalidation = zone low minus 1 ATR; first target = nearest resistance; risk and reward in %, reward to risk after 0.3% round-trip cost. Sell plan = tranches at the nearest and strongest resistance zones; hold the rest above the top zone plus 1 ATR.
5. Plan A: wait for the touch and a 1h close above the zone. Plan B: a 1h close under invalidation cancels the idea. Plan C: near a scheduled event or a leak/trap flag, do nothing until an hour after the official release.
